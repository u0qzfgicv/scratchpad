# Threat Model Template

Quick template for ad-hoc threat modeling sessions.

## Assets
- What are we protecting?

## Trust boundaries
- Where does data cross trust levels?

## Attack surface
- Entry points, protocols, interfaces

## Threats (STRIDE)
- Spoofing, Tampering, Repudiation, Info disclosure, DoS, Elevation

## Mitigations
- Controls, detection, response

## Open questions
- Unknowns to chase down
